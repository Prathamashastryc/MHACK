03
Multi-signal scale recovery

Recover scale by cross-checking.

Three independent, low-cost signals are fused so the pipeline degrades gracefully instead of failing silently.

  • Monocular metric depth plus IPM
  • Reference-object calibration
  • Visual odometry or structure-from-motion

04
Corridor transect sampling

Measure the road locally.

Frames are map-matched to the road graph, then perpendicular transects measure width along the corridor instead of averaging the whole road.

  • Graph-aware map matching
  • Perpendicular cross-sections
  • Curves and tapers preserved
  • Multi-pass coverage

05
Robust aggregation

Let the majority win.

Multiple transects and passes are combined with robust statistics and a smoothness prior so one occluded observation cannot dominate.

  • Median and percentile fusion
  • Outlier down-weighting
  • Multi-angle observations
  • Gradual-width prior

Hackathon-scale implementation

Pragmatic stack, honest boundaries.

Start with lightweight pretrained models and existing public data, then improve incrementally as better sensors become available.

01

Segmentation

Fine-tune SegFormer-B0 or use promptable segmentation for a fast road-surface demo.

02

Metric depth

Use a pretrained metric-depth model and inverse perspective mapping for the first scale signal.

03

Odometry + graph

Use OpenCV tracking, GPS matching and OSMnx to chain observations along the road graph.

04

Satellite + hazards

Consume existing satellite and hazard feeds for fallback coverage and impact translation.

!

Not engineering certification

Confidence describes measurement reliability, not a legal or engineering-grade road certification.

◌

Satellite is a fallback

Satellite-derived width is intentionally coarse and lower-confidence than ground measurements.

≋

Forecasts are external

The system consumes existing flood or hazard forecasts instead of inventing physics-based prediction.
